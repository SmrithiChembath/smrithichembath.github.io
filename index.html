NLP · Explainable AI

Fraudulent Job Posting Detector

Fake job postings are rare, which makes them easy for a model to ignore. I fine-tuned DistilBERT on 18K real job postings where only 5% are fraudulent, and paired every prediction with an explanation a reviewer can check.

  • 90% precision: when it flags a posting, it is almost always right
  • 76% recall of fraudulent postings, with PR-AUC 0.86 on a heavily imbalanced dataset
  • Outperformed a TF-IDF baseline, and SHAP highlights the words behind each flag

Limitation: at 76% recall, about 1 in 4 fraudulent postings still gets through, so this works best as a first-pass filter that routes suspicious postings to a human reviewer, not as an automatic block.
